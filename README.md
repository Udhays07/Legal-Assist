# LEGAL ASSIST

## Overview
LEGAL ASSIST is a digital Know-Your-Rights (KYR) framework designed to improve legal awareness and access to justice. The system provides simplified legal information through a chatbot-based interface to help citizens understand their rights and legal remedies.

## Problem Statement
A large section of Indian citizens lack awareness of their legal rights due to complex legal language, fragmented resources, and limited accessibility. This results in exploitation, unreported violations, and reduced access to justice.

## Solution
The project proposes a digital legal assistant integrated with a structured KYR framework. It simplifies central and state laws into easy-to-understand content and guides users through rights, procedures, and legal aid options.

## Features
- Chatbot-based legal awareness
- Structured Know-Your-Rights framework
- Multilingual and inclusive access
- Admin-managed legal content
- Privacy-first design

## Technology Stack
- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Backend: Python (FastAPI)
- Database: MongoDB (Beanie ODM)
- Chatbot: Rule-based knowledge-driven system

## Roles
- Admin: Manages legal categories and KYR content
- User: Interacts with chatbot to understand legal rights
